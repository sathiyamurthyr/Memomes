How Zero-Knowledge Protection Works

Conventional clouds hold the master decryption key to all your files. In Memomes, encryption happens entirely inside your browser before a single byte touches the network.

01

Key Derivation

Your device runs PBKDF2 with 120,000 iterations using SHA-256 and unique per-file salts. Keys are computed in RAM and never saved or transmitted.

02

AES-256-GCM Seal

Your document, image, audio, or video file is sealed into a binary ciphertext blob inside your browser sandbox using authenticated Galois/Counter Mode.

03

Opaque Storage

Only encrypted gibberish is transmitted to Backblaze B2 decentralized servers via signed S3 PUT requests. Object keys and filenames are opaque hashes.

04

Ephemeral Access

Recipients decrypt on-the-fly via self-destructing share links with anti-screenshot shields, dynamic viewer IP watermarks, and auto-burn timers.

Technical Specifications

Memomes operates under zero-trust assumptions. Even with a subpoena or physical server access, server operators see only opaque AES ciphertext blocks with zero mathematical ability to derive plaintext.

  • Symmetric Cipher: AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode 128-bit auth tag)
  • Key Derivation: PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 120,000 iterations, 32-byte CSPRNG salt)
  • Signing Protocol: AWS Signature Version 4 HMAC-SHA256 presigned PUT
  • Storage Architecture: Backblaze B2 Decentralized S3-Compatible Storage
  • Deployment: Global Edge Anycast CDN on Vercel Infrastructure

What does "Zero-Knowledge" actually mean?

It means the service provider (Memomes) has zero mathematical capability to decrypt your files, view your file names, or read your metadata. Your password and encryption keys exist solely in your browser's RAM.

Can Memomes recover my password if I lose it?

No. Because of our strict Zero-Knowledge guarantee, there is no "Forgot Password" backdoor on our servers. During onboarding, you are provided with an emergency cryptographic Recovery Phrase that only you possess.

Where are the files physically hosted?

Encrypted blobs are stored in enterprise Backblaze B2 data centers with 99.999999999% durability and multi-region redundancy. All files are fully encrypted before reaching cloud storage.

Can I share files securely with people who don't have an account?

Yes! Memomes generates end-to-end encrypted sharing links where the decryption key is passed in the URL fragment (`#key=...`). The key never touches our server, and recipients can view files in their browser without signing up.
